# Roadmap Vision — AlleZwembaden.nl

> **Document Purpose (Step 1.5 of [`PHILOSOPHY.md`](file:///Users/sramzzs4d/Projects-sramzz/swimming/PHILOSOPHY.md#L99)):** Define **what** outcomes we want to achieve across each horizon of AlleZwembaden.nl—without locking implementation details into the vision.

---

## North Star Vision

Become the **#1 trusted Dutch starting point for everything related to swimming**—where parents find the fastest, skin-friendliest *Zwem-ABC* path for their child, lap swimmers verify today's *banenzwemmen* hours and best-value passes in seconds, and open-water swimmers check safe seasonal water conditions across the Netherlands.

---

## Horizon 1: Trust, SEO Authority & Demand Capture (Day 1 Launch — 0 Paying Partners)

**Goal:** Launch a blazingly fast, mobile-first directory that ranks at the top of Dutch Google Search and captures verifiable parent and swimmer demand at **€0 operating risk**.

* **Gold-Standard Flagship Coverage (Randstad / G4 + Key Hubs):**
  * Deep, verified profiles for Amsterdam, Utrecht, Rotterdam, Den Haag, and major regional hubs (including basin-level temperatures, *Zoutelektrolyse* vs. *Klassiek Chloor*, *Banenzwemmen* timetables, *Stadspas/U-pas* discounts, and *Zwem-ABC* waitlists).
* **National Baseline Coverage (~480+ Dutch Locations):**
  * Every Dutch municipality (*gemeente*) and official open-water swimming location represented with accurate coordinates, facility classification, and water quality status.
* **Dutch Calendar & Weather Intelligence:**
  * Automatic awareness of Dutch public holidays (*Feestdagen*), regional school vacations (*Regio Noord, Midden, Zuid*), and *KNMI Code Oranje/Rood* weather advisories so swimmers are never misled by stale regular schedules.
* **Honest Day-1 Value & Demand Proof:**
  * Interactive admission cost calculator (*losse entree* vs. *10-badenkaart*) with tracked outbound links to official pool ticket portals.
  * Native *"Kortste Wachttijd Vergelijker & Alert"* service that helps parents find the 3 fastest-starting *Zwem-ABC* schools near their postcode while building our proprietary dataset of parent demand per postcode.

---

## Horizon 2: Contextual Monetization & First B2B Swim School Partners

**Goal:** Turn organic SEO traffic and verified parent demand into recurring revenue without compromising user trust.

* **B2B Swim School Partner Onboarding ("Warm Wedge"):**
  * Approach commercial swim schools in high-demand postcodes with hard proof (*"64 parents in your postcode requested Diploma A waitlist comparisons this month"*).
  * Enable claimed partner profiles (`PILOT_PARTNER` $\rightarrow$ `PAYING_PARTNER`) with direct intake lead routing and live spot availability (*"Direct instromen: nog 3 plekken op woensdagmiddag"*).
* **Contextual Affiliate Gear Activation:**
  * Activate the stubbed equipment recommendation slots with genuinely useful, context-matched gear (skin-friendly anti-chlorine care for chlorine pools, NRZ training aids for *Zwem-ABC*, and temperature-tiered wetsuits & tow-floats for open water).

---

## Horizon 3: Market Standard & Historical Data Moat

**Goal:** Compounding data advantage that cannot be replicated by generic directories or AI scrapers.

* **Multi-Year Dutch Swimming Data Moat:**
  * Historical tracking of municipal swimming tariffs, seasonal waitlist curves per city, and open-water temperature trends across years.
* **Self-Service B2B Partner Portal ("Word Aanbieder"):**
  * Allow verified swim schools and municipal operators to update their remaining lesson spots and seasonal holiday rosters directly.
* **Sister Domain Expansion (`ZwemLocaties.nl`, `Zwemkaart.nl`):**
  * Leverage the same underlying dataset and engine across specialized Dutch entry points.
